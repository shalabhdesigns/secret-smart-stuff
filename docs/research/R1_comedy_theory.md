# R1 — Comedy theory and joke construction (verified 27 Oct 2026)

Labels: [F] research finding · [E] expert/theory · [H] hypothesis.

Incongruity-resolution
- Two stages: notice an incongruity, find the rule that makes sense of it; some residual absurdity stays. [E]
  Forabosco, *Encyclopedia of Humor Studies* (SAGE 2014): https://sk.sagepub.com/ency/edvol/embed/encyclopedia-of-humor-studies/chpt/cognitive-aspects
- Eye-tracking: at the punchline listeners shift from the first to the final interpretation; non-funny re-thinks do the
  same → reinterpretation is necessary but not sufficient. [F] Israel, Konieczny & Ferstl 2022, *Front. Commun.* 6:
  https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fcomm.2021.758173/text
- "Incongruity" alone wrongly predicts that amazing or tragic events are funny. [F] Warren & McGraw 2016, *JPSP* 110(3):
  https://experts.arizona.edu/en/publications/differentiating-what-is-humorous-from-what-is-not/

Benign violation
- Humour = a violation appraised as benign, at the same time; benign via a competing norm, weak commitment, or distance. [F]
  McGraw & Warren 2010, *Psychological Science* 21(8): https://leeds-faculty.colorado.edu/mcgrawp/pdf/mcgraw.warren.2010.pdf
- Review: violation + benign + simultaneity distinguish humour; surprise and superiority do not. [F, authors' own theory]
  Warren, Barsky & McGraw 2021, *PSPR* 25(1): https://experts.arizona.edu/en/publications/what-makes-things-funny-an-integrative-review-of-the-antecedents-/
- Critique: ignores teller/listener disagreement, power and social context. [E] Kant & Norman 2019, *Front. Psychol.*:
  https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.01380/full
- Distance: severe violations are funnier when distant, mild mishaps funnier when close. [F] McGraw et al. 2012:
  https://leeds-faculty.colorado.edu/mcgrawp/pdf/mcgraw.warren.williams.leonard.2012.pdf ; humour about a tragedy rises
  then falls over time (sweet spot). [F] McGraw, Williams & Warren 2014: https://experts.arizona.edu/en/publications/the-rise-and-fall-of-humor-psychological-distance-modulates-humor/

Script opposition (SSTH/GTVH)
- A joke text is compatible with two opposed scripts; GTVH resources: script opposition, logical mechanism, situation,
  target, narrative strategy, language. [E] Raskin, Hempelmann & Taylor 2009: https://jltonline.de/articles/03.2/raskin-understand-assess-abstract.en.pdf ;
  Chłopicki 2003 review of Attardo: https://www.academia.edu/24580095 ; Attardo, *Humorous Texts* (2001) on line position
  and "strands": https://linguistlist.org/issues/12/1605/

Timing (mixed)
- 20 joke performances: no pause before punchlines. [F, null] Attardo & Pickering 2011, *Humor* 24(2):
  https://lair.etamu.edu/chssa-faculty-publications/8/
- 828 stand-up sets: pauses ~36% longer before high-surprise lines (1.37 vs 1.01 s); pause length predicts audience votes
  (partial r=.36). [F, correlational] Ma et al. 2026, CogSci: https://escholarship.org/content/qt58t1w6r7/qt58t1w6r7.pdf
- Stillness before punchline; close-ups go with more laughter. [F, preprint] https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21803

Repetition, three, callbacks
- Repetition-shift (≥2 similar events then a contrasting one) appears in 36% of story jokes and more in higher-rated ones. [F]
  Loewenstein & Heath 2005, CogSci: https://escholarship.org/content/qt96p2r8qs/qt96p2r8qs.pdf
- Seeing the punch word minutes earlier makes the joke funnier (running gag); seeing it immediately before spoils it;
  easier-to-read text is funnier. [F] Topolinski 2014, *Cognition and Emotion* 28(5): https://kups.ub.uni-koeln.de/43406
- Callback jokes enjoyed and shared more; ~8 s vs ~3 s of laughter in specials. [F, unpublished]
  https://www.fuqua.duke.edu/duke-fuqua-insights/how-marketers-can-harness-power-of-callbacks

Game / status [E]
- Ground who/what/where, find the first unusual thing, return to the game ~3 times, heightening. UCB via Wean, Script Mag:
  https://scriptmag.com/features/improvising-screenplays-finding-funny-screenplay-using-game-scene
- Every interaction as a status see-saw (Johnstone, *Impro*), untested: https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/once-more-with-feeling/201705/every-human-interaction-explained

Relatability / sharing
- Funniness and "relates to me" correlate across meme types (n=225). [F, weak] Jach et al. 2026:
  https://czasopisma.bg.ug.edu.pl/index.php/CIiPP/article/view/14130
- High amusement raised share intent via arousal (one lab ad). [F] Berger & Milkman 2012 (see R4).

Caveat: Western/Chinese samples, text or stand-up, not Hindi animation. Validate with retention/share data.
Not verified: SEP "Philosophy of Humor", relief theory primary sources, Raskin's exact overlap condition.
