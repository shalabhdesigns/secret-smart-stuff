YouTube Shorts
- Shorts up to 3 min since 15 Oct 2024; recommendations for longer Shorts "in the coming months". [O]
  https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/tall-updates-coming-to-shorts/ ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070
- Views count when a Short starts (from 31 Mar 2025); old metric = "engaged views", used for YPP. [O via press]
  https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/03/26/youtube-shorts-views-counting-stats/
- From 24 Aug 2026 views count on start in all formats; earnings use engaged views/watch hours. [O]
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2991785
- Average view duration is computed from engaged views; compare retention with your 10 latest similar-length videos. [O]
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9313698
- Shorts feed has no thumbnails: "viewed vs swiped away" replaces CTR; underperformers stop after the test audience but
  "may gain traction on a later day". [O] https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-march-4-2024/
- Each Short is tested to find people who enjoy it; volume and posting time barely matter (except news); don't delete
  and re-upload. [O via press, 2023] https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/
- Test & Compare can't A/B Shorts. [O] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714
- >60 hashtags → all ignored; ≤3 shown near title. [O] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658

Research on sharing and attention
- High-arousal emotions (awe, anger, anxiety; amusement in the lab) increase sharing; sadness reduces it. [F]
  Berger & Milkman 2012, JMR 49(2): https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/Virality.pdf
- In video ads, surprise concentrates attention and the rate of change in joy retains viewers: open with a quick surprise,
  several short bursts of joy beat one long one. [F] Teixeira, Wedel & Pieters 2012, JMR 49(2):
  https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/emotion-induced-engagement-in-internet-video-ads/ ;
  https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/creating-online-ads-we-want-to-watch
- STEPPS (Social currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public, Practical value, Stories). [E] Berger, *Contagious* (2013),
  https://www.dallasnews.com/business/2013/03/24/business-book-review-contagious
- Video length: one survey experiment found an inverted U peaking ~35 s (~44 s with music) [F, stated intention, n=155]
  https://archives.marketing-trends-congress.com/2025/pages/PDF/060.pdf ; watch time is biased by duration and platforms
  correct for it [F] https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.06003 → length is an open question; test it.
- Popularity is partly luck/social influence; single videos are noisy. [F] Salganik, Dodds & Watts 2006 (Science):
  https://acawiki.org/Experimental_Study_of_Inequality_and_Unpredictability_in_an_Artificial_Cultural_Market ;
  Weng et al. 2012: https://cnets.indiana.edu/news/competition-among-memes-in-a-world-with-limited-attention
- India: 588 M short-video users in 2025 (61% of internet users). [F, industry survey]
  https://manifest-media.in/digital/040226/61-internet-users-in-india-consumed-short-video-content-in-2025-repo.html
- Not found: peer-reviewed "3-second hook" studies for Shorts; official definition of "swiped away"; Hindi-specific
  retention data; creator experiment sample-size guidance.

Decisions taken: judge videos by engaged views, viewed-vs-swiped, average % viewed and shares per 1k engaged views at
48 h and 7 d against the channel's own median of the last 10–20; read the retention curve per beat; test one variable
at a time in alternating blocks of ≥5 videos with a pre-registered decision rule; length starts at ~55 s (owner's choice)
with a planned 35 s vs 60 s block test.
