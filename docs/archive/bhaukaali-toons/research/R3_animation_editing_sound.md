# R3 — Animation performance, storyboard, editing, sound, captions, loops (verified 27 Oct 2026)

Labels: [F] research finding · [E] expert/practitioner · [S] primary standard · [H] hypothesis.

Animation
- Anticipation directs attention; omitting an *expected* anticipation creates a surprise gag. [E] Thomas & Johnston,
  *The Illusion of Life* (1981) p.51 via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anticipation_(animation)
- Staging = "presenting an idea so that it is unmistakably clear"; exaggeration = "accentuating the essence". [E]
  Lasseter 1987 via SIGGRAPH HyperGraph: https://education.siggraph.org/static/HyperGraph/animation/character_animation/principles/prin_trad_anim.htm
- Takes: anticipation 4–6 fr, accent (Warner "pop" in 1 fr), settle ~7 fr @24fps; double take turns 3 fr apart. [E]
  School of Motion: https://www.schoolofmotion.com/blog/how-to-animate-character-takes
- Road Runner falls: the beat before impact is ~31–43 frames in the films (more than 1 s). [F, frame counts]
  https://www.brianlemay.com/Pages/animationschool/animation/lipsyncbook/timingiseverything.html ;
  Jones' rules ("more humiliated than harmed", audience sympathy with the Coyote): https://www.mentalfloss.com/entertainment/tv/chuck-jones-rules-writing-road-runner-cartoons
- Lip sync: open 1–2 fr before audio; hold M/B/P ~2 fr; push shapes on accents; don't pose every syllable. [E]
  https://www.pluralsight.com/resources/blog/software-development/proven-tips-animating-believable-lip-sync
- FACS (Ekman & Friesen 1978/2002) is used by animators; genuine smile needs the eye (AU6+12). [F/E]
  https://www.paulekman.com/facial-action-coding-system/ ; Buchanan, *Animation Studies* 2009: https://journal.animationstudies.org/article/id/30/
- Caveat: universality of basic emotion faces is contested (Barrett & Gendron 2014) → context and dialogue carry meaning. [F]
  https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/500592

Storyboard / editing
- Pixar iterates story reels (boards + scratch voice + music) many times before animation; Disney storyboards since 1933. [E]
  https://bu.edu/bostonia/summer14/pixar/
- Murch's Rule of Six: emotion > story > rhythm > eye-trace > 2D plane > 3D space. [E]
  https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/cutting-on-the-blink-editing-tips-from-walter-murch
- Kuleshov effect exists but is modest; strongest after negative contexts. [F] Barratt et al. 2016, *Perception* 45(8):
  https://research.cbs.dk/en/publications/does-the-kuleshov-effect-really-exist-revisiting-a-classic-film-e-2/ ;
  Calbi et al. 2017, *Front. Psychol.* 8:1684: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5632723

Comic timing / sound
- 828 Chinese stand-up performances: pauses lengthen before high-surprise punchlines; timing features predicted
  appreciation better than semantic incongruity. [F, correlational] Ma et al. 2026, CogSci: https://escholarship.org/uc/item/58t1w6r7
- Stillness before the punchline (body movement vs laughter, r=−0.75 across 24 topics). [F, weak] https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.21803
- Speech vs background: ≥10 LU below speech for music (≤15), ≥15 LU for ambience; non-experts want ~4 LU more. [F]
  Torcoli et al. 2019, AES paper 10175: https://salford-repository.worktribe.com/OutputFile/1489990
- Music drop before a punch, sparing SFX: craft consensus, no controlled study found. [H]

Captions
- 69% watch sound-off in public, 25% even in private; 80% of caption users are not hearing-impaired (US, n=5,616,
  self-report). [F] Verizon Media/Publicis 2019: https://www.streamingmedia.com/Articles/News/Online-Video-News/80-of-Video-Caption-Users-Arent-Hearing-Impaired-Finds-Verizon-131860.aspx
  (The popular "80% more likely to finish with captions" claim is NOT in this source.)
- Netflix: ≤42 chars/line, ≤2 lines, ≤20 cps adults / 17 children; in-time within 1–2 fr of audio, out ~0.5 s after,
  never across a cut; min 5/6 s, max 7 s per event. [S]
  https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/217350977-English-USA-Timed-Text-Style-Guide ;
  https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-General-Requirements ;
  https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051554394-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-Subtitle-Timing-Guidelines
- Captions help comprehension/attention (Gernsbacher 2015 review) but evidence is partly from L2 learners/self-report. [F, contested]
  https://www.learningandthebrain.com/blog/video-captions-benefit-everyone-an-investigation/

Loops / platform
- Since 31 Mar 2025 a Shorts view counts on every start or replay; "engaged views" keep the old definition and drive
  earnings/YPP. [S via press] https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted
- Seamless loop craft: identical first/last frame, loop-designed audio or a breath/silence at the seam. [E]
  https://blog.motionarray.com/learn/davinci-resolve/loop-video-davinci-resolve/
- No peer-reviewed evidence found that seamless loops raise replay or retention. [H]
