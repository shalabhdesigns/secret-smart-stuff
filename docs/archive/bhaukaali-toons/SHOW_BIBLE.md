# Sab Theek Hai — show bible

The writer, stager and judge read this every episode. Supporting people, places and history live in the world memory
(`creative/world.json`); the owner's standing lessons in `creative/lessons.json`.

## The show
- ~55-second 2D cartoon sketches, one a day, on **Bhaukaali Toons** (YouTube Shorts; Reels later).
- Hindi/Hinglish with a light UP (Lucknow/Kanpur) flavour.
- Everyday middle-class India: family, flatmates, neighbours, office, money, festivals, phones. A small situation everyone
  recognises goes wrong because nobody can say no, nobody admits a mistake, and everyone insists "sab theek hai".
- For ordinary Indian viewers 18–45, small towns and cities. They should think "yeh toh mere ghar mein hota hai" and send it
  to the exact person it reminds them of.

## Owner's standing rule
- Genuinely laugh-out-loud funny, relatable, clear on first view, worth sharing and rewatching. Filling the calendar is not
  the goal.
- Story first: use the people and places the story needs; reuse them when they fit, never force them; create new ones
  when needed and keep their continuity.
- Topical episodes (festivals, events, trends) are welcome when they give a fresher angle; mix with evergreen stories.

## Main characters (designs are locked; never change them)
### Dev (mid-20s, IT job, mustard hoodie, office ID badge, round glasses)
- **Wants:** to keep everyone happy and avoid conflict. **Flaw:** cannot say no, cannot complain, apologises when wronged.
- **Lens:** every situation = "how do I get out of this without anyone being upset?"
- **Voice:** soft, hesitant, quick to agree; English office words when nervous ("actually", "no issues", "chill hai",
  "process hai"). Outer voice calm; **inner voice** (panic spiral, ≤3 per episode) braver and louder.
- **Sounds like:** "Haan haan, bilkul… koi baat nahi." / "Wo actually… main kal pakka bata dunga." / inner: "Dev, bol de!"
- **Never:** mean, rude, or cool under pressure; never wins by confrontation (he wins by accident, or doesn't).

### Meera (mid-20s, Dev's flatmate; black tee, checked pyjamas, teal hair streak, nose stud, always eating chips)
- **Wants:** peace, snacks, and to be proven right without trying. **Flaw:** lazy, zero tact.
- **Lens:** "Iska seedha matlab kya hai?" — sees through everyone instantly.
- **Voice:** deadpan, very few words, chewing; her one line lands the joke or twists the knife. Not cruel — just right.
- **Sounds like:** "Toh?" / "Mujhe kya." / "Ye sealed nahi tha, Dev."
- **Relationship cue:** they share a rented flat as friends/flatmates (not a couple); they call each other by name;
  rent, chores, fridge, Wi-Fi and the landlord make it obvious.
- **Never:** romantic with Dev; never the butt of the joke for long; at most 3 lines unless she leads the story.

### Rocky (40s, barber "Bhaukaal Hair Studio", pink shirt, black apron, moustache, belly) — recurring, not regular
- **Wants:** to be the neighbourhood expert on everything. **Flaw:** never admits a mistake; turns every disaster into
  "style".
- **Voice:** loud Kanpur showman — "guru", "kaahe", "ekdam", "bhaukaal hai", "tension na lo".
- **Use only** when the story is about a haircut, his shop, the market or his "expert advice".

## Supporting cast and places
- See `creative/world.json` (who exists, drawn or voice-only, fixed voices, relations, what happened before).
- A person physically present is drawn on screen; voice-only people are on a phone or clearly elsewhere.
- New people get one fixed voice forever and stay consistent with their first episode.

## Comedy rules (full version: docs/craft/WRITER_CARD.md)
1. Hook in the first line; no greetings.
2. One game, heightened about three times; a laugh at least every ~8–10 s; a turn; the biggest laugh near the end; a
   button that loops back to the first line.
3. Natural, complete Hindi/Hinglish; nobody explains the joke; nobody names their emotion.
4. Smart, not loud: laughs come from character logic and status flips, not shouting.
5. Nothing mean about caste, religion, region, gender, body or disability; no politics, no real people, no brands.
6. Every episode is new: a new situation and a new joke engine.

## Example of tone: Episode 1 "The Haircut" (approved)
- **Rocky:** Lo guru! Dekho zara… ekdam Korean hero! Bhaukaal hai, bhaukaal!
- **Dev:** Haan… achha hai… bahut… alag hai.
- **Dev (inner):** Dev! Office mein client ko 'No' bol deta hai… aur naai se darr gaya? Bol!
- **Dev:** Bhaiya… bas side se… thoda sa… kam kar dijiye?
- **Rocky:** Thoda kam? Ho jaayega! Tension kaahe lete ho guru… Baal hain, sarkari naukri nahi.
- *(black: snip snip)* **Rocky:** …Oho. **Dev:** …Achha hai.
- **Meera:** Baal katwaane gaye the… ki daan karke aaye? **Dev:** Kal jaake theek karwa lunga. **Meera:** Usi se?
- **Dev:** Haan… warna wo bura maan jaayega. *(loops to)* **Rocky:** Aao guru! Baitho… aaj kya karein?
