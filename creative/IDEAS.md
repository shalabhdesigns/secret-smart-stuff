**How ideas keep coming (365+):**
- Ideas come from the engine: **arena × pressure × Dev's move × twist**. 40 arenas × 8 pressures ×
  6 moves × 6 twists gives far more than 365 distinct combinations.
- Each candidate is scored on the scorecard. Only ≥ 28/40 is produced.
- Viewer comments ("do one about…") and analytics feed the next batch.
