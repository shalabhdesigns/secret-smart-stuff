## 0. Two platform facts that change every niche score

### 0.1 YPP / Shorts revenue rules from 1 Feb 2027 (about 3 months from today)
Source: YouTube blog and Tubefilter (10 Aug 2026), and ppc.land. All OBSERVED.
- **Shorts ad revenue needs 10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days.** This is checked monthly. A channel below it stays in YPP but earns no Shorts ad or Premium revenue that month.
- **New YPP applicants (Shorts path): 20M Shorts views in 90 days plus 1,000 subscribers.** Today the bar is 10M.
- **New YPP applicants (long-form path): 8,000 watch hours in 365 days.** Today the bar is 4,000.
- **Expanded / fan-funding tier is unchanged:** 500 subs plus 3M Shorts views in 90 days (or 3,000 watch hours). This tier unlocks memberships, Super Thanks and shopping, but **not ad revenue**.
- New bonuses for channels below the threshold: shopping bonuses, brand-deal incentives and "trend" boosts. **No details have been published yet.**
- YouTube VP Amjad Hanif, on small channels: "a few thousand views… a few cents for that month" (ppc.land, 10 Aug 2026).
- **What this means (ESTIMATE):** A new channel starting now will almost certainly be judged under the 2027 rules. A Shorts-only plan needs about 20M views per 90 days to join, and 10M per 90 days to keep earning. That is about 110K views a day, sustained. A hybrid plan is more realistic: long-form compilations of Shorts that build watch hours. Story series and quizzes both compile into long-form easily. Satisfying content does too, but it is high-risk under policy.

### 0.2 "Inauthentic content" policy (renamed 15 Jul 2025; clarified 16 Jul 2026)
Source: official YPP policy page, support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (fetched 2026-10-24). OBSERVED wording.
- **Allowed:** "Similar content, like a series following a set of characters across episodes." Each video should have "a distinct storyline, focus, or concept."
- **Not allowed:**
  - "Videos where characters are put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome."
  - "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative, commentary, or educational value."
  - "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production."
- **July 2026 clarification** (TechCrunch 20 Jul 2026; Tubefilter 13 Jul 2026; Dexerto 15 Jul 2026), three non-monetizable buckets:
  1. Generic or repetitive content.
  2. "Unsatisfying or off-putting" content: emotionally manipulative formulas, for example a repetitive series of animals in exaggerated distress, or disturbing themes with no story arc.
  3. AI personas posing as experts on sensitive topics.
  - A channel dominated by distress-style content is removed from YPP whether or not it uses AI.
  - Example case: HPad, an appliance-destruction channel (225K subs), was demonetized under the "unsatisfying" bucket.
- **Enforcement record:**
  - Jan 2026: 16 of the top 100 AI-slop channels were hit (about 35M subs, 4.7B views) (Soap Central, 30 Jan 2026, citing Kapwing).
  - Named removals: CuentosFacianantes (5.95M; AI Dragon Ball stories), Imperio de Jesus (5.87M; AI religious either/or quizzes and serialized stories) and Super Cat League (4.21M; AI cat content) (OutlierKit; letsdatascience 28 Jan 2026).
  - OutlierKit's summary of what the removed channels had in common: AI did scripting, voice, visuals and publishing with **no human editorial input**.
  - The owner's plan of approving each video, writing original scripts and building an original world matches the "allowed" side. It does not guarantee safety (ESTIMATE).

### 1.4 Retention, loops and comments
- **No public retention or loop data was found** for quiz Shorts (Typito and Growtharo checked, no metrics).
- My assessment (ESTIMATE, unverified): the countdown → reveal structure creates replay loops, and "comment your score" prompts drive comments.
- Engagement where observed is low:
  - Quiz Blitz latest video: 0.7% engagement (vidIQ).
  - MindYourLogic Shorts: 0.01% engagement, but 238 comments/video (Qoruz).

### 1.5 Monetization notes
- **Shorts RPM for quiz Shorts:** "around 10–20 cents" per 1K (Growtharo, Sep 2024). ESTIMATE from that source.
- **Made-for-kids (MFK) consequences** (official Google help 9527654, OBSERVED): no comments, notification bell, personalized ads, end screens, memberships, miniplayer, or save-to-playlist. Google: "may result in a decrease in revenue."
- Many quiz channels are kid-skewed (Quiz Kingdom "for children"; Super Quizone tagged Kids; MindYourLogic Stories "aimed at both children and adults").
- If the content is judged MFK, the **comment-engagement advantage disappears**. Adult-targeted topics avoid this: brands, movies, travel, food, 90s/00s nostalgia, "if you're a girl can you name these brands" (938K views on TriviawithIbiza).
- **Sponsorships:** none detected on Quiz Blitz.

### 1.6 Content gaps (ESTIMATE)
- An adult-targeted quiz with a recurring AI host or narrator persona and running "score" continuity.
- Picture riddles built from original AI scenes ("spot the impossible thing"), rather than stock logos and flags.
- Weekly trend quizzes on new releases (pop-culture timing).
- Weak spots in the current field: low-production emoji/flag templates dominate, and most legacy channels are decaying.

### 2.4 Recurring formats
- Animal-as-human melodrama (monkey, cat)
- Pets vs. wild animals
- Parody reality shows
- Fan-IP mashups

### 2.5 Content gaps (ESTIMATE)
- **An original, brand-safe world with real arcs.** Episodes should have distinct outcomes, which the policy explicitly allows, and avoid distress-bait.
- Interactive elements: viewer votes on what happens next, combined with Shorts series playlists.
- Adult or general audience (25–44, like Aiwhiskertube) rather than kids.
- Most competitors are "slop" with the same setup every time, which is exactly what is now being demonetized.

### 4.3 Hindi audience size and competition
**Size (OBSERVED, mostly secondary sources):**
- India has about 491–500M YouTube MAU in 2026, the largest national base (colorwhistle.com, aggregating DataReportal 2026; second-hand).
- Shorts reached 1 trillion (1 lakh crore) views in India (Benzinga, undated, quoting Neal Mohan).
- India Shorts drew 30B views/day in 2022 (Rest of World, 19 Jan 2023).
- More than 11,000 Indian channels had over 1M subs as of Dec 2023, up 50% year on year (blog.youtube, 7 Aug 2024).

**Competition density (OBSERVED):** Hindi faceless fact, riddle and story channels show **large subscriber counts but very low current reach**. That is a saturated, decayed field:
- World Facts Hindi: 528K subs, avg views 390 (Qoruz, Oct 2026)
- VK Hindi World: 768K subs, 111 videos, latest 237 views (vidIQ, 2026-10-23)
- MindYourLogic Shorts: 2.77M subs, −10K subs/28d (vidIQ)
- DS Kahani Toons: 213K subs, 5K views/28d (vidIQ)
- Live Hindi Facts (1.02M subs) is still active at 6.73M views/28d, est. $2K/mo (ESTIMATE). It has moved to business case studies.
- GK Drishti: 1.93M subs, +2.66% subs in 30d (vidIQ, Jun 2025)

**Fast-growth history:** In 2022–23, Hindi "fact" Shorts channels grew explosively:
- RG Factboy: +4M subs in about 10 weeks, to 11M, then deleted
- MrFactVoice22: +6M in 3 months
- Fx Facts: 7M, then banned

Four of the top 10 fastest-growing channels in Jan 2023 were new Indian fact-Shorts channels (Rest of World). Many were terminated for spam, deception or copyright.

**2025–26 Hindi fast-growth example:** Bandar Apna Dost (AI character, near-wordless humour). No verified 2025–26 *faceless Hindi fact* newcomer with fast growth was found before the search budget ran out.

**Bottom line:** Hindi gets views relatively easily, but competition is dense and enforcement history is heavy.

### 4.5 Captions and readability (Devanagari vs Latin)
- Hindi YouTube auto-captions are 55–85% accurate. Hinglish causes mid-sentence script switching and English words written in Devanagari (notelm.ai; low-quality source, own tables inconsistent).
- Devanagari conjuncts and matras commonly break in video tools without complex-text shaping. Search results showed bug threads for Kdenlive, Premiere Elements and VLC; the threads themselves were not read.
- **ESTIMATE:** Devanagari runs about 10–20% wider and needs a larger font for the same legibility on a phone. Burned-in captions must be rendered with a HarfBuzz-shaped renderer (for example FFmpeg libass with a proper Devanagari font). Many Hinglish channels caption in romanized Hinglish for speed.

### 4.6 Which path is faster to YPP, and which earns more
- **Faster to view thresholds:** probably Hindi/India. India is the largest market and Hindi Shorts historically get high volume. However, many viewers already follow decayed incumbents. ESTIMATE, not proven.
- **Earns more per view:** English-global, by about 3–40× (AIR; see 4.1).
- **Under the 2027 rules,** reaching 10M–20M views/90d at India RPM still yields almost nothing (about $80 per 10M). Fast YPP entry in Hindi does not mean income.

### 4.7 English first, Hindi later: is it a known strategy?
- **Multi-language audio tracks support Shorts.** The official Google help page (13338784, fetched 2026-10-24) says tracks can be added to "a single video or Short". It requires Advanced features, an audio file of roughly the same length, and desktop Studio.
- In Feb 2023 this was long-form only, with Shorts "being tested" (TechCrunch). Creators get "over 25% of their watch time" from non-primary-language views (Google help).
- **Auto-dubbing** covers 27 languages; Hindi gets "Expressive Speech" (Storyboard18, 5 Feb 2026). **Whether auto-dub applies to Shorts was not confirmed.**
- AIR (Jul 2025): auto-dubbed engagement is about 5× lower than professional voice-acted localization; use auto-dub to explore, not to scale.
- **Precedents:**
  - MrBeast stopped his 24M-sub Spanish channel and moved to audio tracks: "much easier to just run one channel than 12".
  - AIR client Watch Me found "separate language channels for each market perform best" (12 channels reached 100K, 4 reached 1M).
  - AIR advises localizing only once the main channel grows steadily (Tubefilter, 1 Aug 2023).
